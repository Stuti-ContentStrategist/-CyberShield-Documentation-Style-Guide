# Tone & Voice

## Overview

CyberShield documentation maintains a **professional yet approachable** tone. The goal is to help users — from IT administrators to developers — quickly understand features, configurations, and troubleshooting steps without unnecessary complexity.

Our voice reflects the product’s personality: **clear, confident, secure, and user-focused.**

> 💡 **Tip:** Always write as if you are guiding a colleague — friendly, respectful, and focused on clarity.

***

### Tone Guidelines

| Trait            | Description                                                | Example                                                                 |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Professional** | Maintain accuracy and credibility; avoid slang or humor.   | ✅ “Restart the agent to apply new settings.”                            |
| **Supportive**   | Empower the reader with solutions.                         | ✅ “If the scan fails, verify your network credentials before retrying.” |
| **Direct**       | Use active voice and short sentences.                      | ✅ “Click **Save** to apply changes.”                                    |
| **Neutral**      | Avoid personal opinions, exaggerations, or marketing tone. | ✅ “CyberShield encrypts all outbound data.”                             |
| **Inclusive**    | Use neutral language; avoid gendered pronouns.             | ✅ “They can configure their MFA settings.”                              |

> ⚙️ **Note:** When giving instructions, prefer the imperative tone — “Click,” “Select,” “Open,” rather than “You should click” or “You will open.”

***

### Voice Principles

1.  **Clarity Over Cleverness**\
    Write for understanding, not for effect. Avoid unnecessary metaphors or marketing language.

    > 🚫 Avoid: “CyberShield is your knight in shining armor.”\
    > ✅ Use: “CyberShield protects your network with real-time threat analysis.”
2.  **Empathy for the Reader**\
    Acknowledge user context when describing solutions.

    > “If this issue persists, check your system logs or contact support@cybershield.com.”
3.  **Action-Oriented**\
    Start steps or instructions with verbs.

    > ✅ “Open the **Dashboard**.”\
    > 🚫 Avoid: “The user can open the Dashboard by clicking...”
4. **Consistency in Terminology**\
   Use the same term across all pages — for example, always use **Dashboard**, not “Control Panel” or “Admin Screen.”

***

### Example Rewrite

| Weak Writing                                                      | Improved Writing                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| “We’ve added a brand-new alerting feature that’s super powerful!” | “CyberShield now supports customizable alert policies for real-time monitoring.” |
| “Now you can easily monitor everything!”                          | “Use the **Dashboard** to monitor threat activity in real time.”                 |

By following these tone and voice guidelines, every CyberShield document stays clear, consistent, and aligned with our brand personality.
